How EdKernel works for teachers

A curriculum-agnostic AI tutoring platform. Teachers set it up once — the AI handles one-to-one tutoring at scale.

🏫

One-time setup (15 min)

Teacher creates the class, adds topic names, and uploads PDFs. The AI reads them automatically — no tagging or formatting needed.

🤖

AI does the tutoring

The AI asks Socratic questions, gives hints when stuck, and checks handwritten working step by step — so teachers aren't needed for every question.

📈

Students learn at their own pace

Each student gets personalised hints based on their specific mistake. They can practise any time, on any device.

📊

Teacher stays in control

The progress dashboard shows mastery per student per topic — so teachers spend their time where students actually need them.
